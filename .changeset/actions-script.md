`vos actions script <actions.json>` prints the flow as numbered beats in plain words, with the holds the script asked for, for a person who will record it by hand in their own signed-in browser: the last rung of the session ladder, handed over as the agent's knowledge of the flow rather than an apology. A step's caption leads its beat, a text selector is said as its words, and an id is said as a name.
