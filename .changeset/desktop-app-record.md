`vos record --app <name>` records a desktop app's window on macOS while you, or your agent's computer use, work the app: the window alone, with no system pointer in its pixels, and the pointer's path, presses and typing activity beside it, planned like a page take. `--window <id>` names one window, `--windows` lists what can be recorded, `--seconds` gives a length, and `vos doctor` says whether Screen Recording and Accessibility are granted.

A take can say how round its footage's own corners are (`meta.cornerRadius`), and the card is then at least as round, so a recorded window's corners do not show as black notches.
