What a second film across three apps found, on the release.

- `vos digest` looks inside a long wait. An idle of 45 s or more carries `glance` moments: the seconds where the picture changed while nothing was pressed (strongest first, kept apart), then an even pace through what is left, each with a frame. An 18 minute task used to digest to one idle moment and no picture.
- `vos validate`: a typed field is read where its caret was, from the take's own `key` events. A terminal is pressed at its middle and typed into at its top, and typing inside a play's step is not a `type` step; both were told their zoom points beside what was clicked, and a caption anywhere over a terminal was told it sits on it.
- `vos validate` and `vos join` warn when a film's inputs were recorded under two appearances (`meta.appearance`, which `@vosjs/driver` 0.4.1 writes).
- A `wait` or an `expect` may carry `"zoom": false`: the wait is watched whole, and the planner proposes nothing over it, as over a play's stretch. `"zoom": true` on one is still refused.
- `vos plan --motion --kicker "…"`: a film with no brand and no release takes the kicker as the line under its end card's title. It used to be laid nowhere.
- The CLI bundles skills 0.32.1.
