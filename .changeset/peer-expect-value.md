A peer's `expect` keeps what its check last answered on its step (`meta.peers[].steps[].value`), as the take's own steps do: when the check reads the app's state, the value says what the peer saw at that moment, which no frame of the take can, since the peer's page is never filmed. JSON, at most 2 KB, else that JSON cut short as a string.
