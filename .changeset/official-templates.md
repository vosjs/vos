A template ref resolves on vos.so's official shelf by title as well as by id or slug (`--with "End card"`, `--style "Split cover, landscape"`), and the end card `vos plan` proposes when `LAUNCH.md` leaves `endCard` on or unsaid is the official `End card` template, laid at the end and stamped `from: endcard`; the house clips stand in offline. A template stamped `from: endcard` is the end card: `endCard: none` drops it and a re-plan replaces it.
