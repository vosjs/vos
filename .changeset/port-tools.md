Bringing a finished piece into vos has its tools. `vos port inventory <page|project>` reads a HyperFrames composition, a hand-rolled page or a Remotion project's render as a browser renders it (words with their computed faces, sizes and colours, the palette from custom properties, faces matched to the catalog, media with their windows, the scenes a timeline reveals, the CSS no element can say, the words and faces a canvas script paints) into `port/inventory.json`, laying each HyperFrames word out where it settles in its scene, with stills from its render. `vos port scaffold` turns that into a data-first program: every word a bound text element, every colour a data key, the knobs over them, a label and a TODO per scene with each scene's words shown in its window, a painter starter only where needed, the score as a `doc.json` track, and `port/REPORT.md`. `vos build <program.mjs>` turns real functions into `config.json`, refusing a module-scope read, syntax the page cannot run and non-JSON data, with `config.sources.json` mapping each function to its line. `vos compare <program> --against <render>` scores every sampled frame (SSIM) and writes source | vos | difference sheets, exiting 1 when any frame is under the threshold. The bundled skills are 0.13.0, whose `vos-port` procedure uses them.
