A freeze is a retime primitive beside the speed spans: `doc.freeze` holds source moments frozen for output seconds, anywhere in the footage, placed by the lowering as rated pieces from one seam (`placeFreezes`), drawn by a `freezeLane` on the same row as the speed bands, and read as the rest where the last one begins. A segment's `hold` is the legacy spelling, migrated on read into a freeze at that segment's end (hosted documents step to schema version 4). The CLI's schema and lints take `freeze`, `plan --reuse` re-times freezes like the manual spans, and `plan --style <poster>` carries the trailing freeze.
