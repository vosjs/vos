A brand kit can name the hue its callouts speak in. `vos callout` coloured the kicker and the card from the kit's `accent`, and some brands reserve theirs: a recorder's red marks time and nothing else, so a note kicker in it broke the brand's own rule, twice over when the product's playhead was in the same frame. `BRAND.md` may now carry a `callout` role, which wins over `accent` (`--accent` still wins over both). The composer cannot know what a brand reserves; a role is how the kit says it.
