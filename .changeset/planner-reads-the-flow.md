The planners read the flow's own structure. On a scripted take, a `wait` step between the first and the last doing step, or a hover's dwell, in which the digest's activity measured nothing moving is proposed as a speed span at 2× (`speedParams.stillRate`; only with the activity witness, so a page loading behind a wait is never compressed, and never the opening or closing wait, the clip's establishing and resting beats), the presses inside one step (a click and the dialog it opened, a drag's presses) join one zoom while their targets share a window, and a park before the first step is never a dwell. `vos plan` and `vos digest` hand the planners `meta.steps`.
