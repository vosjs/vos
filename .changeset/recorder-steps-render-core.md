The recorder takes four new kinds of step: `expect` checks the page on camera, `await` waits off camera so the footage runs straight across a slow result, `download` keeps a file the page makes, and `"guard": "spend"` holds a step that costs something real until the recording names it; a click on a disabled control is reported, and `hostedRefusals` says what a hosted recorder cannot run.
