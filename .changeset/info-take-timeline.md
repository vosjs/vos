`vos info <take> [--json]` prints the take's two clocks: per media, every step with its id, its source seconds (`tStart`..`tEnd`), its caption and where it plays in the cut; then the output timeline after trims, speed and freezes (segments, speed spans, freezes, zoom and tilt spans, every layer with its anchor resolved, sound clips, the still and the end), read through studio-core's own functions.
