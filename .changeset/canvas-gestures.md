`actions.json` speaks canvas. `press` takes `keys`: a chord (`"Shift+D"`, `"Mod+Z"`, with `Mod` Meta on macOS and Control elsewhere), a plain key (`"r"`) or a sequence (`["v", "Escape"]`), each a typing ping that never carries the key. `drag` takes `modifiers` held for the whole drag, and `path`: viewport points inline or in a file beside the actions file, replayed point by point by their own times or evenly over `ms`, drawn with a pen when the points carry pressure. `click` takes `count: 2 | 3`, `button: "right" | "middle"` and `modifiers`, and `click` and `hover` take a viewport point (`x`, `y`) instead of a selector. A new `paste` step pastes words through the browser's clipboard with a trusted chord, and an image file as its own bytes in a paste dispatched in the page (the clipboard re-encodes a picture and drops its density); a hosted take refuses it. A gesture may `settle` (per step, or for the whole script): it waits until the page has made no DOM change and painted no new frame for 300 ms since its last change, and goes on at once on a page already still. A paced gesture that ran more than a fifth past its script is reported as `meta.steps[].overrunMs`, `overruns` in the done event and one line. `vos actions script` says the new steps in words. The recorder's driver contract gains `mouse.down`/`up` options, `keyboard.down`/`up` and an optional `grantPermissions`; `RecordOpts.files` reads the files a script names. `StepSpan` gains `overrunMs` and `settleMs`. The script a take keeps is self-contained: a path a file held is written inline in the take's `actions.json` (what a push embeds, a fetch writes back and a hosted take runs), `--hosted` sends it inline, and a pasted file is kept beside the script as `paste-<name>`.
