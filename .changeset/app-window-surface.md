A take can be one application's window. `captureSurface: 'app'` says the frame is the window and the cursor is already in the window's own points, so the take keeps its cursor, plans its zooms and opens with no browser bar, since the footage shows the window's own title bar. `meta.app` names the application where a page take names its URL, `producer` gains `'desktop'`, and the digest reports the surface and the app's name.
